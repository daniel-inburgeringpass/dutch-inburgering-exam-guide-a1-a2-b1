## 📖 The 5 Inburgering Exam Parts Explained
1. **KNM (Knowledge of Dutch Society):** 45 minutes, 40 multiple-choice questions about Dutch norms, values, institutions, and daily rules.
2. **Lezen (Reading):** 110 minutes, 25 multiple-choice questions based on letters, manuals, notices, and short articles.
3. **Luisteren (Listening):** 45 minutes, 25 multiple-choice questions with short video and audio clips.
4. **Schrijven (Writing):** 40 minutes, 4 open writing tasks (filling out a form, writing a short email or note).
5. **Spreken (Speaking):** 35 minutes, recording spoken answers directly on a computer.
